### Hi, I'm Alvanza (`@oszavla`) 👋

I am a **Graduate Student in Theoretical Physics** focusing on the intersection of **Quantum Field Theory in Curved Spacetime** and **Relativistic Quantum Mechanics**. Alongside research, I build scientific simulations, mathematical visualizations, quantum information, and structured physics education materials.

---

### 🔭 Research & Academic Focus

- **Black Hole Perturbations & Curved Spacetime QFT:** Investigating quasi-bound states of massive Dirac fermions in Schwarzschild spacetime—combining analytical WKB approximations (energy spectra and decay widths) with numerical shooting-method solvers, as well as Primordial Black Hole ("Dark Atom") phenomenology.
- **Quantum Computing & Error Correction:** Implementing and verifying multi-qubit quantum circuits and quantum error-correction protocols using **Qiskit**.
- **Classical & Quantum Foundations:** Analytical mechanics (Hamilton-Jacobi formalism, canonical transformations) and canonical quantization of electromagnetic radiation fields.
- **Physics Education & Outreach:** Authoring high-school and undergraduate problem sets, illustrated solution modules, and technical seminar decks in Classical Mechanics, Electrodynamics, and Calculus.
